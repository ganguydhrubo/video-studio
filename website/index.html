What do I need to get started?+

A Windows PC and your clips or photos. Takebloom checks your hardware and suggests suitable settings. Processing speed depends on your footage and computer.

Does everything work offline?+

Core video editing runs locally. Downloads, updates and cloud AI need internet access. Some local AI features require separate models or dependencies.

Can every PC generate AI video?+

No. Local AI clip generation needs a supported NVIDIA GPU and substantial VRAM. Takebloom checks compatibility. It does not promise generated video on every PC.

Is the screen above the actual app?+

It is an interactive website concept that explains editing, colour and framing. The scenes are original Indian-perspective concept visuals. The desktop app has its own interface and capabilities.
