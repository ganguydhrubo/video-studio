## Takebloom 1.9.0 — drop it in, it edits itself

Takebloom is now one thing, done properly: **editing the footage you shot**. Drop your clips, pictures and a song together and the film starts by itself. Say what you want in your own words, including motion graphics and 3D titles, and Takebloom does it. It learns from every edit you make. Everything still runs on your PC, free, with no account.

This release also includes 1.8.1, which was never published on its own (Download and Share, ready posts for seven sites, Instagram-style covers, iPhone HEVC previews).

### Automatic from start to finish
- **One drop for everything.** Clips, pictures and one song go in the same drop. Five seconds after the files are ready, the edit starts by itself. Click in the prompt box to describe it first; the countdown waits.
- **It tries again by itself.** If an edit stops, it is retried once from the step where it stopped (finished steps are reused).
- **Covers by themselves.** A reel cover (9:16), a profile-grid cover (3:4) and a YouTube thumbnail (16:9) are made from the finished film. Covers you make yourself are never replaced.
- **Your pictures in the edit.** Pictures are placed in quiet moments with a slow pan and zoom, never over your big text moments. Pictures alone make a photo film.
- **Your song is the music.** A dropped song is used as the soundtrack.

### Say what you want
- **The prompt sets the edit.** Everyday words set the format, length, colour look, pace, motion, effects, transitions, captions and music: *"widescreen YouTube video, black and white, no captions"*. A "Takebloom will" card shows what was understood before anything starts, and a "Cannot do yet" list says honestly what is not possible.
- **Motion graphics and 3D.** *"3D title that spins"*, *"neon name tag with my name"*, *"kinetic text"*, *"end card"*, *"confetti at the end"*, *"floating 3D hearts"*, *"sparkles"*, *"light leaks"*. 3D titles are real 3D letters that fly in, spin and catch the light. Graphics never cover your big text moments, extra clips or pictures, and captions step aside for titles. Made on your PC with HyperFrames and three.js; works offline. "No graphics" turns them off.
- **Learns from every edit.** Your choices, refines, the lines you keep or cut, downloads, shares, covers and stars are kept in one file on your PC. When your prompt says nothing about pace, captions, music, colour, transitions or effects, Takebloom uses what you usually choose and says so with the real count. What you write or pick always wins. "Forget what you learned" is on the System page. No AI is trained.

### Better edits
- **Smoother joins.** At a change of clip or moment, the sound leads or trails the picture a little (J-cuts and L-cuts). Write "hard cuts only" to switch them off.
- **Voice exactly in sync.** Fixed: your voice could run up to a tenth of a second early by the end of an edit.
- **Captions on the word.** Windows-voice captions now match the speech (they ran later and later along each line). Karaoke highlights wait through gaps and no longer light up on silence.
- **Fix it on the timeline.** After an edit, "Fix on the timeline" lets you trim, reorder and delete pieces; move or delete pictures and B-roll; edit, retime or delete captions; and set the music level, with undo. "Make the film again" re-cuts only what changed and never runs speech recognition again. The edit is saved as a standard HyperFrames file in the project.
- **Best quality cut-out.** A new "Best quality" choice for background removal gives cleaner hair and clothing edges (BiRefNet lite, MIT). Its 214 MB model downloads only when you tick the box, and the time is estimated from your own PC first. It is much slower than Fast, which stays the default.
- **Free stock clips, optional.** With your own free Pexels or Pixabay key, Takebloom can add stock cutaways. Only a few search words leave your PC, and every clip is credited in `exports/credits.txt`. Without a key nothing is searched.

### Faster and easier to set up
- **About a third faster.** The style pass uses all your processor cores with the same picture, footage reading and voice clean-up run side by side, and the final video uses your PC's fastest working hardware encoder after a short one-time test.
- **One-click setup on a bare PC.** Setup installs everything editing needs even with no Python, FFmpeg or Node on the PC: FFmpeg, a private Python with speech-to-text, Node.js and HyperFrames. You see each download's size and licence first, each is checked before use, it needs no admin rights, and a stopped download continues where it stopped. Blender, the bigger speech model and the offline AI brain are optional.

### Removed
- **Script to video and AI clip** were removed from the app. Takebloom now focuses on editing the footage you shot; generating new footage needed paid services or a large NVIDIA card.

### Your AI choices are unchanged
The free AI editors (OpenCode, Ollama) and your own Claude Code, ChatGPT/Codex, Gemini and Qwen accounts all still work. AI stays optional; with AI Off everything stays on your PC.

Checked so far: 378 Python tests and the file-input test (also with only Pillow and NumPy installed, as CI runs them); the installer built locally (30.4 MB) and the frozen app passed the release smoke check. These real runs went through the app window, on a 13th-gen i7 laptop with Intel graphics and 16 GB:
- an edit of 2 clips + a picture + a song with the prompt "9:16 reel, 3D title that spins, neon name tag, warm look, confetti at the end": the countdown paused for the prompt, the card listed every wish, and the film was ready in 7 min 9 s with QA 100/100, the 3D title, name tag and confetti in place, the picture placed, the song used, one L-cut, and three covers made by themselves;
- a picture-only film that started by itself (13 s), a restyle (16 s) and a Fast cut-out (41 s);
- the Best quality model download from the tick box (214 MB, checksum checked).
Each area was also run for real on its own branch before merging (speech, speed, cut-out, learning, setup on a PC with every tool hidden, timeline, J/L-cuts, motion and 3D).

Not checked: a truly clean PC or virtual machine (the bare-PC setup was tested on this laptop with every tool hidden), a live Pexels or Pixabay search (no key on the test PC), NVIDIA and AMD hardware encoders, and Best quality cut-out on real footage of a moving person.
