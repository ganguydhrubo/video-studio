## Takebloom 1.8.0 — more ways to make a video, on any PC

Takebloom now checks the PC it is running on and says what it can make there before a job starts. It also makes photo videos, restyles videos, removes backgrounds, and updates itself safely. Everything runs on this PC; nothing new is sent anywhere.

- **What this PC can make.** A new card on the System page reads the real hardware: processor, memory, free space, graphics card and its memory. It also tests each hardware video encoder with a one-frame encode instead of trusting the list. From this it gives the largest practical resolution and frame rate, a comfortable job length, an expected time, and which features are ready, slow, need a free add-on, or need a cloud service. The same check runs before each job. It warns about limits and refuses only what the PC truly cannot do. It works on any PC, from an 8 GB laptop to a workstation with a large NVIDIA card. When nothing is chosen, the quality is set for the PC.
- **Photos to video.** Pictures get slow pan and zoom moves on a blurred fill, so tall and wide photos both fill the frame. Then come transitions, a colour look, titles (optionally read aloud by the offline voice) and a music bed cut to the beat.
- **Restyle a video.** Change the colour look or the frame shape (blurred fill or crop), or the speed with pitch-correct sound. You can also add smooth slow motion, a steadier camera, clean grainy footage, sharpen, or add music.
- **Remove or replace the background.** A person cut-out on the processor, with smoothing over time and edge clean-up. The new background can be a blur, a plain colour, a picture, another video, or transparent (WebM, or ProRes 4444 .mov for other editors).
- **AI clip.** Text-to-video and picture-to-video with an open model (Wan 2.2, Apache-2.0), only on PCs with an NVIDIA card of 8 GB or more (12 GB for picture-to-video). The several-GB model downloads only when you allow it. On other PCs Takebloom says plainly that a cloud service is needed, and you can edit the result here.
- **Templates, looks and transitions.** 24 production templates (reels, ads, explainers, weddings, travel, real estate, music videos and more) set format, pacing, captions, music, look and transitions. Anything you choose yourself still wins. There are 16 colour looks, made as real 3D LUTs, and 57 transitions in 13 families. They are in Create and on the command line (`video-studio library`).
- **Safe in-app updates.** Check, download, install and go back to the earlier version from the System page. Each download is checked against the release's published SHA-256 before it can run. Installing is refused while an edit runs. The installer keeps the previous copy, and your projects and settings are never touched. With automatic checking on, an update downloads by itself but never installs by itself.
- **Logo on the taskbar.** The app window now shows the Takebloom logo and name on the taskbar and title bar instead of the Edge globe, and keeps it after reloads.
- **New commands.** `photos`, `restyle`, `cutout`, `generate`, `machine`, `library` and `update`.

Checked before release: 150 Python tests and the file-input test. These real runs were done on a 13th-gen i7 laptop with Intel graphics and 16 GB:
- a 4-picture photo video with glitch transitions, a teal-orange look and titles (18 s to make);
- a restyle to 16:9 noir at 0.75 speed, steadied;
- background cut-outs to blur, colour and transparent on a 6-second 1080×1920 clip;
- the AI clip refusal on a PC without NVIDIA;
- the update check against the live release list;
- photo, restyle and AI-clip jobs through the app server;
- the new screens in a browser at desktop and phone width.

Not checked on real hardware: AI generation on an NVIDIA card, a real update install, and a rollback (see Known limitations).
