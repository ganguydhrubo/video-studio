## Takebloom 1.7.0 — your whole message is kept

Fixes the edit that turned a 52-second talking-head video into 15 seconds. The brief "best taliking head wit 2 sec hook" was read as "make a 2-second video", so the editor threw away 7 of 9 lines. A time next to a hook, intro, title, transition or outro now times that part only.

- **Brief reading.** Whole-video length and part timings are kept apart, including typos ("hok", "intor"), spelled numbers ("two sec"), and Hindi and Bengali digits and words. "Remove 2 seconds from the start" is a trim, not a length. A short time with no clear owner ("make a 3 sec") keeps the whole recording and asks a question instead.
- **Full message by default.** Without a request for a shorter video, every spoken line stays in recorded order, spoken words such as "Actually" stay, and an AI editor's cut that drops lines is refused. Joined lines no longer replay the same instant twice.
- **Old projects repaired.** A project saved with the hook time as its video length is corrected the next time it is planned. A length you typed yourself is never changed.
- **Instructions assistant.** While you type, the AI editor fixes spelling, says what the edit will do, and offers up to three optional ideas and real questions. Nothing replaces your text unless you click "Use corrected wording". With AI off, the same safe reading runs on this PC.
- **AI that answers.** When Google refuses a Gemini CLI sign-in, the message now says so plainly and the next free AI (OpenCode) is tried first for six hours. The project AI budget is 12 calls.
- **Honest labels.** A technical score of 100 now reads "Technical checks passed", not "Ready to publish". Opening tips are suggestions, not audience claims.

Checked before release: 67 Python tests and the file-input test. A real brief check through OpenCode's free model in English and Bengali. The real 52-second project replanned on a copy: 9 of 9 lines, about 51 seconds, hook 2 seconds. The original project and its exports were not changed.
