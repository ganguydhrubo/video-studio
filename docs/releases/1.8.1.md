## Takebloom 1.8.1 — download, share and covers, and iPhone videos that play

- **Download in one click.** The finished film now has a Download button next to Play, and a Share button.
- **Share tab.** Download the video, the 16:9 version, the covers and the captions (.srt), or open the folder with the file selected. "Share with apps on this PC" opens the Windows share panel (WhatsApp, Mail, Nearby sharing and other apps that take videos).
- **Ready posts for seven sites.** Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and WhatsApp each get a caption you can edit, with hashtags taken from what was said, and short steps. "Open <site>" copies the caption and opens that site's upload page. Takebloom does not sign in to your accounts or post for you: posting by itself needs each site's own approval.
- **Instagram-style covers.** Pick a frame, write a title, choose a style and colour, and see it as a reel cover (9:16), inside a 3×3 Instagram profile grid (3:4) and as a YouTube thumbnail (16:9) at once. Save makes 1080×1920, 1080×1440 and 1280×720 JPEGs; the reel cover becomes the video's thumbnail and the automatic one is kept.
- **iPhone videos preview again.** HEVC .MOV files that showed only sound (0×0) now get a small H.264 preview copy made on this PC. The edit still uses your original file.
- **Script videos get real post text.** The title, description and hashtags now come from the script instead of being empty.
- **Never a stale page.** The app page is no longer cached, so an update always shows the new screens.
- **New README** with screenshots and an honest comparison.

Checked before release: 158 Python tests and the file-input test. Real runs on the release-test laptop: an iPhone HEVC .MOV preview copy (16 s); covers saved at all three sizes; a 22.5-second script video with voice, music and captions made in 18 s with a quality score of 100; and the Share tab in Edge with no page errors.

Not done: Takebloom cannot post to social media by itself. See [Known limitations](../KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md#sharing-and-covers-181).
